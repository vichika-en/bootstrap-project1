ASUS ROG Strix G16

ROG Strix G16 (2025)
    • Uses Intel Core Ultra 9 275HX (24 cores / 24 threads).
      GPU: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 Ti with 12GB GDDR7.
      Display: 16″, 2.5K (2560 * 1600), 240 Hz, 16:10, 3 ms, G-Sync, MUX switch.
      Memory: 16 GB DDR5-5600, up to 64 GB.
      Storage: 1 TB PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD.
      Cooling: Tri-fan design + full-width heatsink.
      Battery: 90 Wh, supports USB-C charging (up to 100W).
      Ports: HDMI 2.1, 2x Thunderbolt 5, USB-A, 2.5G LAN.
      Weight / Build: According to 2023 spec sheet it’s ~2.50 kg.

    • The ASUS ROG Strix G16 is a powerful next-generation gaming laptop designed for gamers and creators who need exceptional performance, premium visuals, and advanced cooling in a stylish 16-inch form factor. Built with high-end Intel HX-series or Core Ultra processors and paired with NVIDIA GeForce RTX graphics, it delivers outstanding speed for AAA gaming, video editing, 3D rendering, and multitasking. Its 16-inch 16:10 display — available in high-refresh 165 Hz or ultra-smooth 240 Hz 2.5K “Nebula” options — offers vivid colors, sharp details, and fluid motion, making every game and creative workflow feel incredibly immersive. The Strix G16 also stands out for its advanced Tri-Fan cooling system, liquid-metal thermal compound, and wide exhaust vents, ensuring stable performance even during heavy gaming sessions. With customizable RGB lighting, a solid build, fast DDR5 RAM, expandable storage, and a large 90Wh battery with USB-C charging support, the ROG Strix G16 blends raw power with modern convenience. Whether you're a competitive gamer, content creator, or power user, it offers the perfect balance of performance, display quality, and upgradeability, making it one of the most capable 16-inch gaming laptops in its class.
